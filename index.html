About

I grew up near the Sundarbans in southwestern Bangladesh, where salinity intrusion and long dry seasons decide what people can safely drink. My undergraduate thesis at BUET followed ten household rainwater tanks in Koyra through 122 days of dry-season storage. Water that entered the tanks free of coliforms exceeded the WHO guideline by the October sampling round, and in plastic tanks, clear water was still not safe water. I want to understand how water quality changes between the source and the point of use, and to design low-cost treatment that works where resources are thin.

I am now working on a second kind of water pressure: how much the water use of AI data centres would add to stress in already water-scarce regions of Bangladesh and India.

I also photograph the coastal landscapes and communities whose water problems I study.
