Research

Who bears water risk, measured where people live

My work asks how water risk is distributed in climate-stressed South Asia, and how to measure it locally rather than as averages. My thesis did this for drinking water in one coastal upazila. My current paper does it for water stress from a new industrial demand.

Questions

Water quality between source and point of use

Why does rainwater that is safe at collection become contaminated in household storage, and how much do tank material, handling and storage time matter?

Low-cost treatment for saline coastal settings

What treatment can households in salinity-affected areas afford and keep using through the dry season?

Water stress from new demand

When water-intensive infrastructure such as AI data centres arrives in water-scarce regions, how should its impact be counted so that local scarcity is not averaged away?

Methods I have used: dry-season field sampling of household tanks; pH, turbidity, and total and fecal coliform analysis at the BUET Environmental Engineering Laboratory; a 100-household survey; group comparisons with Holm correction for multiple tests; Python for data analysis.

Current work

Stress-Adjusted Water Impact of AI Inference Sites in Bangladesh and India

Working paper · In preparation

Estimates of AI’s water footprint usually report litres. But a litre drawn from a water-scarce basin costs a community more than a litre from a water-rich one. This paper applies a stress-adjusted water impact measure (Adjusted Water Impact, from the SCARF water-stress framework) to the same large-language-model inference workload placed at hypothetical data-centre sites in Bangladesh and India. The aim is to show how much siting changes the real water burden of identical AI use, and what that means for planning in the Global South.

Variation of Rainwater Quality During Storage in Non-Rainy Seasons: A Case Study in a Coastal Area of Bangladesh

Undergraduate thesis, BUET · Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Md. Abdul Jalil

Ten household tanks in Koyra, Khulna, tracked for 122 days. Coliform-free at the start; above the WHO guideline from October in both brick and plastic tanks. Brick tanks were consistently worse.
